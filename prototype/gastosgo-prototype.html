Open questions this prototype should answer

In Target Mode, is "spending cap" (what you can spend) clearer than "savings target" (what you want to save)? They feel like the same number but mean different things psychologically.
Is 4 steps (amount → category → payment → confirm) the right flow, or should category come first?
Does the gap card's green/red color correctly signal the user's state across all 3 income modes?
Budget impact on the confirm screen — useful signal or noise before saving?
In Mixed Mode, should income records be logged from the same + FAB flow, or a separate income screen?
