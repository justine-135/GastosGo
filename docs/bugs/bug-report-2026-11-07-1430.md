| 1 | Insights / Pie Chart | High | App crashes with `Uncaught Error [Reanimated] Tried to synchronously call a non-worklet function onPress on the UI thread` when tapping a pie slice | 1. Navigate to Insights tab 2. Tap any slice on the donut pie chart |

## Notes

Two compounding root causes:

1. **Missing Babel plugin** — `react-native-reanimated/plugin` is absent from `babel.config.js`. Without it, Reanimated's worklet infrastructure is never compiled in, breaking all gesture callbacks at runtime.
2. **Non-worklet `onPress` on `Pie.Slice`** — `victory-native` v41's `Pie.Slice` calls its `onPress` prop directly on the UI thread. The callback in `app/(tabs)/insights.tsx:183` is a plain JS function and must be wrapped with `runOnJS` to marshal back to the JS thread.

Bar chart (`TouchableOpacity`) and legend (`TouchableOpacity`) are unaffected — they are not Reanimated gesture components.
